## Content Tier Structure

### HIGH-PRIORITY (Posts 1–30)
- Publish first: These are highest-search-volume topics
- Schedule: Every 2–3 days for first 15 weeks
- Length: 1,500–3,000 words
- Include: Case studies, real numbers, calculators

### MEDIUM-PRIORITY (Posts 31–70)
- Publish weeks 8–20
- Length: 1,200–1,800 words
- Include: Data visualizations, comparisons

### FILLER & SEASONAL (Posts 71–100)
- Publish weeks 21–30
- Mix in: Shorter posts (800–1,000 words), niche topics
- Seasonal timing: Wedding budget in June, tax planning in Jan, etc.
