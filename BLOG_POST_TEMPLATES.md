## Writing Guidelines for All Posts

### Tone
- Friendly, non-judgmental
- Empowering (help users make smart financial decisions)
- Transparent (admit limitations)

### Structure
- **Headline:** Must include the benefit, not just the topic
  - ❌ "Using the Mortgage Calculator"
  - ✅ "How to Calculate Your True Monthly Mortgage Payment in 3 Steps"
- **Meta description:** 155 characters max, include CTA
- **Opening:** Hook the reader in 2 sentences
- **Subheadings:** Use H2/H3, include keywords naturally
- **Visuals:** 1 screenshot or chart per section (every 300 words)
- **Closing:** Always end with a CTA linking to the relevant calculator

### SEO Checklist Per Post
- [ ] Title includes primary keyword
- [ ] Meta description written (155 chars)
- [ ] Internal links to calculators (2–4 per post)
- [ ] Internal links to other blog posts (1–2)
- [ ] External links to authoritative sources (1–2)
- [ ] Images have alt text
- [ ] Word count: 600–3,500 words
- [ ] Schema markup added (Article schema)

### Internal Linking Rules
- Every calculator mention gets a link: `[Mortgage Calculator](/mortgage.html)`
- Link to related blog posts: `[Read our guide on home affordability →](/blog/financial-planning-roadmap)`
- Vary anchor text (don't always use the exact calculator name)
